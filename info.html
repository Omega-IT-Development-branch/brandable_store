Brandable is a multi—brand online store presenting swiss and international fashion brands.

Established in Lugano in December 2016, Brandable is a family-operated company specializing in online retailers of high quality and luxury fashion brands for men, women and children. The shop offers a wide selection of clothing, shoes, bags and accessories from various brands, including Hunter, Falke and Hanro and local Swiss brands such as Old Captain Co., Baabuk and LouBay.

On our site you can order everything your family needs: bodies and baby socks, underwear for all ages, shoes for any activity or season, as well as, bags, sportswear, knitwear, exclusive shirts produced in Switzerland and elegant furs.

Brandable was created with three main goals in mind:

1.Brandable was created with three main goals in mind:

Brandable provides Swiss consumers fast, reliable service by handling their goods logistics and warehousing in Ticino. This reduces delivery time and avoids additional costs such as VAT, ensuring customers have a positive buying experience from purchase to parcel receipt.

2.Offer Swiss products and services to consumers abroad with free worldwide shipping.

The quality and prestige of Swiss Made products are highly appreciated by international customers and to meet this ever-increasing demand Brandable offers free worldwide shipping, making a wide range of Swiss brands available to markets previously out of reach.

3.Contribute to the development of the fashion industry in Switzerland.

Brandable, a proud Swiss company, is constantly working towards making Switzerland an important fashion center. One way Brandable is contributing to this effort is by offering unique services to help Swiss start-ups and designers gain recognition through customized services designed to identify the best solutions for the companies individual needs. Such services include, for example, launching them on the Brandable store, posting articles, and promoting their products on social media.

Brandable is committed to offering high quality luxury products and impeccable customer service to customers in Switzerland and abroad. We hope you appreciate the wide choice of products available that will suit you for both personal purchases and the best gift ideas.
